# Euler-Lagrange momentum models (CFDEM A / B / Bfull): plan for the model key and Bfull

Status: PLAN of 2026-11-15, superseded by `el_momentum_models_2026-11-15.md` (what was built,
including the changes the design consultation led to). Kept as the record of the starting point. Worktree `FeatFloWer-mommodels`, branch
`feature/el-momentum-models` (from `feature/el-ergun-column` 1c3ead6c). Owner decisions of
2026-11-15: the three CFDEM model types as a deck switch; Bfull first, then B, then A in operator
form; default unchanged; modular and switchable, no interference with the non-Euler-Lagrange
solution paths. This document covers stage 1 (model key) and stage 2 (Bfull). Stages 3 (B) and 4
(A) are outlined at the end so that stage 1 and 2 do not block them.

## 1. Target equations

Notation: eps porosity, phi = 1 - eps, u interstitial fluid velocity (the solver unknown, as under
`ELVolumeAveraged = continuity` today), n F_d drag per volume, V particle volume.

| | Fluid momentum | Force on a particle |
|---|---|---|
| today (`none`) | rho Du/Dt = -grad p + div tau - n F_d [+ phi grad p with `ELPressureFeedback`] | F_d [- V grad p with `ELPressureForce`] |
| **Bfull** | eps rho Du/Dt = -grad p + div tau - n (F_d + F_p + F_v) | F_d + F_p + F_v, F_p = -V grad p, F_v = V div tau |
| B | eps rho Du/Dt = -grad p + div tau - n F_d / eps | F_d / eps |
| A | eps rho Du/Dt = -eps grad p + div(eps tau) - n F_d | F_d + F_p + F_v |

Continuity in all three: d eps/dt + div(eps u) = 0 (the conservative form, already implemented).
Inertia as in `cfdemSolverPiso`: eps (du/dt + u . grad u), with eps at the new time level.
Pressure is the dynamic pressure: the fluid carries no gravity term, the particle carries
(rho_p - rho_f) V g. For a uniform rho_f this equals CFDEM's full pressure + Archimedes split
(checked with the peer on the start-up case).

Bfull against today's best set (pressure force + feedback + conservative form): two things are
missing, the eps weighting of the inertia and the particle viscous force F_v with its feedback.

## 2. What the code offers (verified in this tree)

- `mgDensity(ILEV)%x(iel)` (`QuadSc_var.f90:337`) is an element-wise density per multigrid level.
  It feeds the lumped mass matrix and its inverse (`Create_MRhoMat` -> `Assemble_Mass_Generic`,
  `QuadSc_assembly.f90:333`), the convection matrix (`Create_KMat` -> `CONVQ2`, `:790`), the body
  forces (`Grav_QuadSc`, `QuadSc_corrections.f90:166-224`), and through `MlRhoPmat` the pressure
  matrix (`Create_CMat`, `:632`) and `Velocity_Correction` (`QuadSc_corrections.f90:12`).
- The momentum matrix is `A = MlRho + theta dt (S or D + K)` and the right-hand side uses the same
  `MlRho` (`Matdef_general_QuadScalar`, `QuadSc_def.f90:2152`). With `mgDensity = eps^{n+1} rho`
  this is eps^{n+1} (u^{n+1} - u^n)/dt + eps u . grad u: the CFDEM form, without touching an
  assembly routine.
- Operators are regenerated by phase: `OperatorRegenaration(1)` at start-up, `(2)` and `(3)` at
  the top of every fluid solve (`QuadSc_main.f90:574`), selected by the deck digits of
  `SimPar@MatrixRenewal` (EL decks: `M1D1K3S0C1`, i.e. mass and pressure matrix once, convection
  every step). Per-step renewal of M and C exists (`C >= 2` re-factorises the coarse pressure
  matrix on the master, `QuadSc_mg.f90:135`).
- The Euler-Lagrange step is its own routine, `Transport_q2p1_UxyzP_el` (`QuadSc_main.f90:345`):
  particle-mesh pass, particle advance, second pass, then
  `Transport_q2p1_UxyzP_fluid_core(..., enable_fbm=.FALSE.)`. No other application calls it.
- Particle forces: `EL_COMPUTE_PARTICLE_FORCES` (`el_forces.f90:32`) builds drag, pressure, lift,
  buoyant weight, `particle_total` and `feedback_force`; grad u and grad p are already sampled at
  the particle (`EL_SAMPLE_GRAD_U_*`, `EL_SAMPLE_GRAD_P_*`).
- Conservative continuity (`el_volume_averaged.f90:69-76`): per source evaluation the residual
  `r_m = F_m(u) + d Q_m` of all four pressure rows is turned into the projection right-hand side
  `defp_m = -r_m / (eps_e dt)`. The division by eps_e is a preconditioner: with unweighted inertia
  a pressure increment q changes eps u by eps dt (rho M)^-1 B q.
- Coarse levels: element values are restricted by the eight-children mean
  (`QuadSc_initialization.f90:664-672`) and the coarsest level is collected with
  `E010_CollectCoarseVector` (`:680`).

## 3. Design

### 3.1 One key, one module

`SimPar@ELMomentumModel = none | Bfull | B | A` (default `none`), table-driven like
`el_va_compat_names` / `el_va_form_names` (`el_config.f90:96-131`), lower-cased, validated against
the table. Stage 1 accepts `none` only and refuses the three model names with "not implemented in
this build"; each later stage unlocks one name.

New module `source/src_el/el_momentum_model.f90` (`EL_MOMENTUM_MODEL`) owns everything a model
changes, behind four small questions and one action. Nothing outside the module tests the model
name:

- `EL_MM_ACTIVE()`: deck-only logical, false for `none`.
- `EL_MM_PARTICLE_FORCE_SET(...)`: which of F_p, F_v the particle receives and what is fed back
  (replaces the two bare `IF (el_pressure_force)` / `IF (el_pressure_feedback)` in
  `el_forces.f90`; for `none` it returns exactly today's keys).
- `EL_MM_DRAG_SCALE(eps)`: 1 for none / Bfull / A, 1/eps for B (stage 3).
- `EL_MM_PRESSURE_PRECONDITIONER(eps_e)`: the factor that turns the conservative residual into the
  projection right-hand side (section 3.4).
- `EL_MM_UPDATE_FLUID_COEFFICIENTS(...)`: sets the fluid coefficient fields for the coming solve
  (section 3.3) and says which operators must be regenerated.

Adding a model later means one table row and one branch per function in this module.

### 3.2 Implied keys and refusals (stage 1 writes the rules, stage 2 activates them)

Under a model key the following are implied, echoed as "implied by ELMomentumModel", and an
explicit contradictory value in the deck is refused at start-up (pattern of `el_config.f90:203`):

| Key | Bfull | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| `ELVolumeAveraged` | continuity | the model is the volume-averaged set |
| `ELVAContinuityForm` | conservative | section 3.4 relies on the flux residual |
| `ELVASourceCorrectors` | >= 1 (deck value kept if larger) | required by the conservative form |
| `ELApplyFluidFeedback` | Yes | |
| `ELPressureForce`, `ELPressureFeedback` | Yes, Yes | the Bfull force split |
| `ELDragCoupling` | semi_implicit | fluid drag relaxation time is far below dt in dense beds |

Refused under any model: `ELDragModel = difelice` (campaign form without eps^2; owner decision),
`ELMesoFilter = Yes`, `ELMomentumFix = Yes` (both act on the unweighted fluid momentum),
`ELEpsFRelax > 0`, a prescribed fluid field, and comm modes other than 3 / 4 (inherited from the
conservative form). `ELVACompatibility` stays a deck key (closed box: uniform or alpha; open
outlet: none), with the existing check in the application.

### 3.3 eps-weighted inertia through the fluid coefficient field

`EL_MM_UPDATE_FLUID_COEFFICIENTS` is called once per step in `Transport_q2p1_UxyzP_el`, after the
last particle-mesh pass and before the fluid core:

1. `mgDensity(NLMAX)%x(iel) = rho * eps_inertia(iel)` on every worker, where `eps_inertia` is the
   stored P0 porosity `epsilon_f` (the conserved variable; no clipping beyond `ELEpsFMin`, which
   the field already carries).
2. Restriction to the coarser levels with the eight-children mean, as at initialisation; the
   coarsest level collected for the master with `E010_CollectCoarseVector` if the master
   assembles velocity operators (to be confirmed in step 2 of section 5: `bMasterTurnedOn`).
3. Regeneration of the mass matrix every step. Implementation choice: the module calls
   `Create_MRhoMat()` itself after writing the field, so the deck's `MatrixRenewal` digits keep
   their meaning and a model run does not depend on the user remembering `M2`. Convection is
   already per step in every EL deck (`K3`); a model run with `K < 2` is refused.

Consequences that follow automatically, and are wanted:
- `Velocity_Correction` divides by the weighted lumped mass: u = u* - dt (eps rho M)^-1 B q.
- Body forces through `Grav_QuadSc` become eps rho g and eps rho f_b (`ConstantForcing`): the
  consistent form. Fluid gravity is normally off in EL decks (dynamic pressure); the periodic bed
  E1 under a model key therefore measures the "consistent" column of its RUNBOOK.

Isolation from the non-EL paths: the field is only written from the EL transport routine and only
when `EL_MM_ACTIVE()`; `mgDensity` keeps its initial constant value otherwise. No shared routine
gains a branch for this. Applications that never call `Transport_q2p1_UxyzP_el` cannot reach the
code; the DNS twins certify it.

### 3.4 Projection with weighted inertia: the pressure matrix stays as it is

With weighted inertia, a pressure increment q changes the flux eps u by
eps dt (eps rho M)^-1 B q = dt (rho M)^-1 B q: the eps cancels. The operator that maps q to the
conservative residual is therefore the UNWEIGHTED pressure matrix C = B^T (rho M)^-1 B that the
code already has, and it is the exact Bfull / B operator up to the lumping (CFDEM: laplacian
coefficient rUA eps with rUA proportional to 1/eps).

Design: keep `mg_CMat` built from the constant-density lumped mass (no per-step rebuild, no
multigrid change, no coarse re-factorisation), and set the residual scaling to 1 instead of
1/eps_e: `defp_m = -r_m / dt`. That is `EL_MM_PRESSURE_PRECONDITIONER = 1` for Bfull and B, and
`1/eps_e` for `none` (today, bitwise) and for A (where -eps grad p brings the eps back).

Needed for this: the unweighted inverse lumped mass must survive the per-step mass regeneration.
`Create_CMat` reads `mg_MlRhoPmat` at build time only, so C built at start-up stays valid; the
per-step `Create_MRhoMat` overwrites `MlRhoPmat` for `Velocity_Correction`, which is what we want.
To be verified in the code before implementing: no other consumer rebuilds C from the weighted
mass during a run with `C1` (Section 5, step 2).

The projection is then, per pass: solve C q = -r(u)/dt, correct u with the weighted mass, update
p. The fixed point (r = 0) does not depend on the preconditioner; the corrector loop
(`EL_VA_Projection_Correctors`) is unchanged. Expectation: the unsinked residual contracts at
least as fast as today, because the preconditioner is now the right operator; this is measured
(audit line `EL_VA_FLUX`, `res1_L1` per corrector) and reported, not assumed.

Alternative considered: rebuild C from the weighted mass every step (`C2`) and keep 1/eps_e. It
costs a pressure-matrix assembly and a coarse factorisation per step and gives a
variable-coefficient multigrid problem. Kept as a fallback if the measurement disappoints.

### 3.5 Particle viscous force (the second missing piece of Bfull)

CFDEM `viscForce`: F_v = V div tau at the particle, fed back to the fluid in Bfull. In FeatFloWer
div tau of a Q2 field is not available as a smooth quantity; element-wise second derivatives are
rough and discontinuous. Proposal: use the discrete weak form the solver already has. The nodal
viscous force density d = -Ml^-1 (S u) (diffusion operator applied to the velocity, divided by the
unweighted lumped mass) is a continuous Q2 field; it is computed once per transfer pass and
sampled at the particles through the existing kernel sampler, like grad p. Open points, to be
settled by the design consultation before this sub-stage is written:
- Dirichlet and wall nodes: the weak form lacks the boundary traction there; mask them (zero
  force density) or extrapolate.
- cost of one extra operator application per pass and one more sampled vector (3 slots).
- the force is O(phi) of a term that is itself small in bed flows; it matters in sheared
  suspensions.

Owner decision 2026-11-15: `Bfull` must be complete. The viscous force is developed as its own
sub-stage (2b) after the weighted inertia and force split (2a), but the model name `Bfull` stays
refused ("not implemented in this build") until 2b is certified. During 2a development the
incomplete set is reachable only through a development switch that is removed before the merge.
Fluid body forces become eps rho g under a model key (owner: agreed; cases are redone if needed).

### 3.6 Diagnostics

- Echo: `EL momentum model = <name>` plus the implied-key lines, on both units.
- New audit line `EL_MM_MOMENTUM` on audit steps: integral of eps rho u (weighted fluid momentum),
  its rate, the deposited feedback split into drag / pressure / viscous, and the weighted body
  force. The existing `EL_FLUID_PAIR` audit is built on the unweighted momentum; under a model it
  is switched to the weighted integral inside the diagnostics module (one function, keyed on
  `EL_MM_ACTIVE()`), and its bytes stay unchanged for `none`.
- `tools/el_stream_compare.py`: tag `EL_MM_MOMENTUM` added.

## 5. Steps and verification

1. **Stage 1, key only.** Config, parser, module skeleton with `none` behaviour, echo. Build
   `build-el` (build-va recipe) and `build-dns`. Gates: ten certified EL configurations and the
   conservative T10a run bitwise (`tools/el_stream_compare.py --strict` where no new token is
   expected), DNS twins e4_l3 and DKT bitwise, negative tests (unknown model name refused; model
   name refused as not implemented).
2. **Code checks before stage 2** (read-only): who consumes `mg_MlRhoPmat` after start-up; whether
   the master assembles velocity operators (`bMasterTurnedOn`) and needs the coarse field; whether
   any routine caches the mass matrix across steps (BDF / Crank-Nicolson right-hand side).
3. **Design consultation** (GPT-6 Astra high, as for the conservative form) on sections 3.3-3.5:
   the cancellation argument and the preconditioner, the time level of eps in the mass term, the
   viscous force density. Findings adopted into this document before coding.
4. **Stage 2a.** Weighted inertia, force split, preconditioner 1. Unit tests: restriction of the
   coefficient field (constant and linear eps reproduced, mean preserved); force set per model
   against a hand table; the 1-D start-up balance of the peer (section 6) integrated with the
   module's force set and mass weights gives tau_A, and tau_unw for `none`.
5. **Probe-first smokes**, 20 steps each, 5 and 28 ranks: tier-2 closed box and the Ergun column
   with `Bfull`; no NaN, residual contraction per corrector reported, rank-independent audit
   values.
6. **Gates at the default**, second round (EL configurations, T10a, DNS twins): bitwise.
7. **Validation runs.**
   - Ergun column E2 (clone of `q2p1_param_E2_cons_pf_fb.dat` with the model key replacing the
     individual keys): slope, U_mf and plateau must reproduce job 159991 within 1 % (inertia is
     negligible there: 12 686 Pa s/m, 0.01375 m/s, 1.02), flux balance 1.00.
   - Periodic bed E1 under `Bfull`: U_sup at the "consistent" reference (eps rho f_b).
   - Start-up of a uniform suspension from rest (peer's case, rho_p / rho_f 1.1, phi 0.4): the
     time constant with `none` + pressure force + feedback must be 1.25 x and with `Bfull` 1.00 x
     the model-A value, terminal slip identical; the `none` run needs no new code and is run first
     as the baseline. Like-for-like numbers against CFDEM when the peer's campaign exists.
8. **Reviews**: GPT-6 Astra high and Fable on the implementation, briefs with the sustainability
   requirement; findings applied; third gate round; record commit and datasheet rows.
9. **Stage 2b** (viscous force) as its own cycle of steps 3-8, with a sheared case added to the
   validation (to be chosen with the peer).

## 6. Predictions the runs are held against

Start-up case, slip s between fluid and particles, beta = drag per volume per slip:

| Formulation | Time constant |
|---|---|
| A, Bfull | eps phi (rho_f phi + rho_p eps) / beta |
| `none` + pressure force + feedback | eps phi (rho_f phi / eps + rho_p eps) / beta (1.25 x at phi 0.4, rho_p/rho_f 1.1) |
| B | eps^2 phi rho_p / beta (0.62 x) |

Terminal slip eps phi (rho_p - rho_f) g / beta in all. Derived by the peer, re-derived here.

## 7. Risks and open points

- **Preconditioner claim (3.4).** If the residual does not contract as predicted at sharp porosity
  jumps (bed top), the fallback is the per-step weighted pressure matrix.
- **Lumped mass with P0 eps.** The nodal weight is the mass-weighted mean of the adjacent
  elements' eps; at a porosity jump the weighted velocity correction and the flux residual use
  different eps representations (P0 against the Q1 transport porosity). The fixed point is
  unaffected; the convergence rate may be.
- **Step cost.** One mass assembly per step on all levels; measured in the smokes.
- **Viscous force near walls (3.5).**
- **Restart.** Nothing new is stored: the coefficient field is rebuilt from the porosity before
  the first fluid solve.

## 8. Outline of the later stages

- **B (stage 3):** `EL_MM_DRAG_SCALE = 1/eps`, particle pressure and viscous force refused and not
  fed back, everything else as Bfull (same weighted inertia, same preconditioner 1). Validation:
  Ergun slope equal to Bfull; start-up time constant 0.62 x.
- **A (stage 4), operator form:** pressure is element-local (P1 discontinuous), so -eps grad p is
  B applied to eps_e p_e: a scaling of the element pressure unknowns in `AddPressureGradient` and
  in the velocity correction, behind the module. The viscous term becomes div(eps tau) through the
  element viscosity coefficient of the diffusion assembly (to be surveyed). The particle receives
  F_p and F_v without feedback. The pressure operator is then the eps-weighted one: preconditioner
  1/eps_e with the existing C, or a weighted C; decided after stage 2 measurements. All changes
  stay inside the EL transport path and the module, as required.
