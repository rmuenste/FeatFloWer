# M1 — Steady DFG 2D-1 cylinder with Chimera-S (q2p1_chimera)

Design: `chimera-integration-design.md` §3 (Phase-3 notes) and §10 row 3.
First executed 2026-09-02. Regression anchor:
`tools/featflower_test/testcases/{definitions,baselines}/q2p1_chimera_cylinder.yaml`.

## Case design

- DFG 2D-1 (Schäfer–Turek) channel 2.2 × 0.41 × 0.05, cylinder centre
  (0.2, 0.2), D = 0.1, ρ = 1, ν = 1e-3, parabolic inflow U_max = 0.3
  (U_mean = 0.2, Re = 20). Steady case; forces normalised with
  2/(U_mean²·D·H), H = 0.05 (`postParams` defaults).
- **The cylinder is not in the background mesh.** Background =
  uniform 44 × 8 × 1 hex channel (`_data/CHIMERA_FAC/chimera_fac.tri`,
  generated by `tools/chimera_meshgen/channel_tri.py`), refined by
  `SimPar@MaxMeshLevel` (h = 0.05/2^(L−1), i.e. D/h = 4·2^(L−2)).
- Atmosphere = one annulus around the cylinder, inner radius r = 0.05,
  width H = 0.05 (r_o = 2r), body table `_data/chimera_particles.dat`
  (`cylinder_z 0.2 0.2 0.0 0.05 0.05 0.0 0.05`). The coarse shell is the
  Phase-2 Couette fixture `annulus_coarse.tri`, fitted affinely to
  [r, r+H] × [zlo, zhi] at load time; refined by `ChimeraSubmeshLev`
  (keep it equal to `MaxMeshLevel` for joint refinement).
- Coupling: Chimera-S (nodal hole/fringe Dirichlet), Robin outer condition
  α = 1 (paper eq. 5d), submesh time-discrete with the background Δt
  (backward Euler, own previous level). The time loop is the outer
  Gauss–Seidel iteration; its fixed point is the coupled steady state.
- Forces from the atmosphere surface stress: `ChimeraForce1:` lines in
  `_data/prot.txt`, columns `time C_D C_L Fx Fy Fz Tz`.

## Staging checklist

1. Build target `q2p1_chimera` (+ `metis`); CMake stages `_data/`
   (deck, body table, channel mesh + `.par` files, annulus shell,
   `MG.dat`), `partitioner/` and `start/` into the build dir.
   `start/data.TXT` + `start/sampleRigidBody.xml` are required by the
   FullC0ntact bootstrap even though no FBM particle exists — a missing
   `start/` aborts at init.
2. Partition into `_mesh/<MeshFolder>/` with the Python partitioner
   (the deck's `SimPar@MeshFolder = "CHIFAC"` must match the folder name):
   ```bash
   cd build/applications/q2p1_chimera
   python3 <repo>/tools/PyPartitioner.py 3 1 1 CHIFAC _data/CHIMERA_FAC/chimera_fac.prj
   ```
3. Deck knobs for the ladder (`_data/q2p1_param.dat`):
   `SimPar@MaxMeshLevel`, `SimPar@ChimeraSubmeshLev` (equal),
   `SimPar@TimeStep`, `SimPar@MaxSimTime`. Everything else stays.
4. Launch with one rank more than partitions (rank 0 = master, holds no
   Chimera state). On the sandbox call `mpirun` by absolute path:
   ```bash
   /sfw/openmpi/gcc14.3.x/4.1.6/ucx-threaded-noverbs/bin/mpirun \
     --mca opal_warn_on_missing_libcuda 0 -np 4 ./q2p1_chimera > run.log 2>&1
   grep ChimeraForce1 _data/prot.txt | tail -1
   ```
5. Sanity lines at init (`run.log`): `Chimera: rank k hole dofs = …,
   fringe dofs = …, max donor extrapolation = …` — extrapolation must be
   0 (fringe donors found inside atmosphere elements); L2: 230/225,
   L3: 1710/837 on the owner rank.
6. `ChimeraEnable = Yes` with FBM particles in the same deck aborts by
   design (M1 restriction).

## Gates

- **Regression anchor** (featflower_test `q2p1_chimera_cylinder`):
  L2/L2, dt 0.05, last line at t = 6.05, tolerance 1e-4 on C_D, C_L.
- **Worker-count invariance**: the anchor must reproduce to all printed
  digits with 2 partitions / 3 ranks (`PyPartitioner.py 2 1 1 CHIFAC2 …`).
- **dt insensitivity** of the steady limit (dt 0.01 vs 0.05 at equal time).
- **Accuracy**: joint-refinement ladder against the body-fitted solver
  (`q2p1_fc_ext_cylinder`, same channel, cylinder in the mesh) and the
  DFG band C_D 5.57–5.59, C_L 0.0104–0.0110 (reference 5.5795 / 0.01062).
- **Off-regression**: `q2p1_fc_ext_cylinder` on the same binary with a
  Chimera-free deck must match its baseline exactly (tolerance 0, no
  Chimera strings in `prot.txt`).

### Reading of the ladder

- Drag error drops 4.4× from L2 to L3 (consistent with 2nd order, but two
  rungs do not establish an order).
- Lift is non-asymptotic at L2: the hole/fringe staircase in the
  background is asymmetric by up to one cell (h = D/4) top vs bottom and
  flips the sign. At L3 sign and magnitude are right (error 25 % and
  decaying at t = 4; the L3 run was not continued to a settled lift).
- The limiting error is the background near the fringe, not the
  atmosphere: refining the atmosphere alone at fixed L2 background does
  not change the lift sign, and the L3 Chimera drag is further from DFG
  than the body-fitted L3 value. This is the expected Chimera-S
  behaviour on coarse backgrounds and the motivation for the weak
  variant (Phase 4) and finer backgrounds.
- Atmosphere-only convergence (Phase-2 annular Couette, `chi-submesh-
  couette`): velocity 2nd order in L2 (measured 2.08, the Q1-geometry
  limit shared with the production discretisation); surface-traction
  torque sub-quadratic, 1.0 % at L3.
